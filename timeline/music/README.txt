Put your music files in this folder (FLAC, MP3, OGG, WAV or M4A).

Then, in timeline-editor.html:
  1. Open your timeline folder (button at the top right).
  2. Go to the Music tab and click "Scan music folder".
     The editor reads each file's own title, artist, album and track number,
     and its properties (for FLAC: sample rate, bit depth, channels, length).
  3. Fix any names or the order if you like, then Save.

The player next to the timeline's title plays the tracks in the order shown in the Music tab.

Tip: FLAC files are big (often 20-40 MB per song). GitHub refuses files over 100 MB,
and very large files take visitors a while to start playing.
